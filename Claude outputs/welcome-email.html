Thank you for confirming. You are on the list for The Furniture Magazine, the trade publication for the people who make, repair and finish furniture in the UK, and the businesses that employ them.

What arrives: a short brief every fortnight. Trade news with the source named, one number worth knowing, one technique from the bench, the live jobs, and the diary. From time to time there is a longer edition.

Two rules govern everything we print. Nothing is made up: every figure has a source and the source is named. Paid content is always labelled. Spot an error and you can reply to any issue; it comes straight to the editor and is corrected in the next one.
